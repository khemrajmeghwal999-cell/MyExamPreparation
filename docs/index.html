About the App

My Exam Preparation is an educational Android application designed to help students prepare for examinations through practice questions, mock tests, previous year questions, study notes, progress tracking and other preparation tools.

Key Features

  • Previous Year Questions (PYQs)
  • Practice Questions and Mock Tests
  • Subject and Topic-wise preparation
  • Study Notes
  • Bookmarks and Wrong Questions
  • Progress and Study Goals
  • Optional Google Drive Backup and Restore
  • Offline-first study experience

Privacy

We respect your privacy. The application is designed as an offline-first study app, with optional Google services for backup and synchronization.

For complete information about data handling, Google services, advertising, storage and user choices, please read our Privacy Policy.
