My Exam Preparation

Effective Date: September 23, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to My Exam Preparation. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information associated with your use of our Android application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our App.

My Exam Preparation is designed primarily as an offline-first educational study app with optional cloud backup and synchronized question bank features.

2. Local Data Storage on Your Device

The App stores certain study data locally on your device using Android's private SharedPreferences and internal file storage. This data includes:

  • Study Progress & Quiz History: Your test attempts, completed quiz sets, scores, and wrong answers for review.
  • Bookmarks & Notes: Questions you save for practice and study notes you create.
  • Study Goals & Streaks: Your daily study target, streak counter, and streak history.
  • App Preferences: Your selected app language (Hindi/English) and theme preference (Light/Dark/System).
  • Ad Cooldown Timestamps: Local timestamp markers to ensure ad frequency limits (such as a 30-minute interval between interstitial ads) are respected across app restarts.

All locally stored data remains stored in private app storage on your device unless you clear app data, uninstall the App, or explicitly perform a cloud backup.

3. Google Services & Cloud Integration

The App integrates specific official Google services to provide optional backup, question synchronization, and advertising functions:

A. Google Sign-In & Google Drive (Optional)

If you choose to sign in with your Google Account, the App uses official Google Play Services Authentication to enable optional features:

  • Personal Backup & Restore: Uploads or restores your encrypted test history and bookmarks to your private Google Drive storage. The App only requests access to files created by the App itself (drive.file / drive.appdata scopes) and cannot access your other personal Google Drive files.
  • Master Question Bank Sync: Connects to a central Google Drive Master Question Bank file to update and download new study questions.

B. Google AdMob (Advertising)

The App uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display banner and interstitial advertisements:

  • AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as Advertising ID), IP address, coarse location data, and app interaction metrics to serve and measure advertisements.
  • Ads are served according to Google's AdMob policies. For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and Switzerland, advertising is conducted in compliance with applicable European privacy and consent guidelines.

4. Over-The-Air (OTA) Updates

The App includes an automated version check feature to inform you when a newer version of the App is available:

  • On app startup or manual request, the App performs an HTTPS GET request to check a version.json file hosted on a public GitHub repository.
  • No personal user identifiers are sent during version checks.
  • When an update is accepted, the file is downloaded using the standard Android system DownloadManager to the app's internal downloads directory, and opened via Android's package installer upon your explicit action.

5. Third-Party Services Summary

The App only integrates third-party SDKs that are strictly necessary for its functionality. We do not include any third-party analytics (such as Firebase Analytics or Facebook SDK), crash reporting SDKs, or social tracking frameworks. The integrated services are:

  • Google Play Services Auth: User authentication for optional Google Drive backup (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Google Drive REST API: Private app backup and question synchronization.
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob): Advertisement serving (Google Ad Technologies).

6. Data Retention & Security

We do not operate external user tracking databases. Your personal study progress remains on your local device or within your personal Google Drive account. We maintain security by utilizing official Android system APIs, encrypted HTTPS network connections for updates, and restricted Google Drive authorization scopes.

7. Your Data Rights & Choices

You maintain full control over your data within the App:

  • Local Data Removal: You can clear all local study data at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing App Storage in your Android system settings.
  • Account Disconnection: You can sign out of your Google Account in the App Settings screen at any time.
  • Advertising ID Reset: You can reset or opt out of personalized ads via your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

8. Children's Privacy

Our App is an educational preparation tool designed for general audiences and student examination preparation. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 years of age.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or regulatory changes. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised Effective Date.
